Frequently asked questions

Are reach mowers and boom mowers the same type of equipment?

The Spider and Scorpion machines are described as reach mowers and boom mowers. Their arms carry interchangeable tools for work such as grass mowing, hedge cutting and tree trimming. Compare the arm, mounting arrangement and cutting tool together.

Which model can work on both sides of the tractor?

The Spider series can position the arm on either side of the tractor and bring the cutting head forward. The range covers Spider 520, 620, 720 and 820 PLUS models; check the mounting configuration on the selected product page.

Can I use a flail mower or a tree-trimming saw on the arm?

DTE’s range includes hydraulic flail mower heads and Quadsaw tree-trimming attachments. Compatibility depends on the arm, tool and carrier. Ask DTE to confirm the combination before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Can these flail mowers be used for verge mowing?

Yes. The FR 92 and FR 112 are designed for verge mowing of grass and thicket. The FR 122 and FR 162 are also used for grass mowing on verges, ditches, dykes and slopes.

Will a hydraulic flail mower fit my tractor or excavator?

These heads can mount on compatible Scorpion boom mowers for tractors or directly on excavator arms, with adapters and brackets for selected arrangements. This is a hydraulic cutting head: confirm the arm, bracket and oil-flow requirements for the selected model.

When should I consider the FR 122 VB?

The FR 122 VB has a hydraulically driven conveyor belt that can run in either direction. It is designed for embankments and ditches beside streams, rivers, canals and dams, directing cut material away from the water.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a high-flow skid steer for the V70 disc mulcher?

Yes. The V70 requires a high-flow loader delivering 30–50 US GPM. Hydraulic flow, pressure, carrier capacity and cab protection all need to suit the attachment. Read the skid steer sizing guide and confirm your exact machine with DTE.

Should I choose a disc mulcher or a drum mulcher?

Disc mulchers suit open rural sites where clearing speed is the priority. Drum mulchers produce a finer finish, contain debris more closely and can grind stumps below grade. Disc mulchers throw debris further, so site conditions matter. Compare drum and disc mulchers.

Can the disc mulcher grind stumps below ground level?

Choose a drum mulcher for below-grade stump work. Review the Virnig drum mulcher range if below-grade work is part of the job.

Frequently asked questions

Which skid steer brush cutter suits uneven ground?

The V50 Floating Deck model follows the contour of the ground for a consistent cut on uneven terrain. It cuts trees up to 4 inches in diameter. Check the product specification and loader fit with DTE.

Will every Virnig cutter run on the same skid steer?

No single hydraulic requirement applies to every model and motor option in this range. Provide your loader make and model, hydraulic flow and pressure, then ask DTE to confirm the selected cutter’s motor and mounting specification.

What cab protection and stopping behaviour should I check?

A shatterproof cab is required on the loader. The V70 Open Front cutter has no hydraulic braking and its flywheel may take minutes to stop. Review the selected model’s operating requirements before use.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Quadsaw be used as a tractor-mounted tree-trimming attachment?

Yes. Quadsaws can mount on tractors and front loaders using compatible boom arms or Multi Carriers. Excavator and telehandler arrangements are available for selected models. Check the exact saw, carrier and mounting combination with DTE.

Which Quadsaw cuts the thickest branches?

The LRS 2402 has the largest maximum branch thickness in this Quadsaw range, at 21 cm. The LRS 1402, 1602 and 2002 are listed at 10, 12 and 16 cm respectively. Use the full product specification when selecting a model.

How have customers used Quadsaws for hedging?

At Yellingbo Olive Grove, Howard Meltzer’s published story describes pruning around 2,000 olive trees in approximately 10 days with an LRS 1602 system. Pinecliffe’s story reports that one operator could complete work that previously needed two or three people. These are individual customer experiences.
