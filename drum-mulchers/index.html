Frequently asked questions

Can the V70 drum mulcher grind stumps below grade?

Yes. It can mulch about 2 inches below grade when rolled forward on its skid shoes. For heavy stump and below-grade work, a variable-speed motor with a smooth or bite control drum is recommended.

Do I need a high-flow skid steer for the V70 drum mulcher?

Yes. The V70 needs a high-flow loader delivering 30–50 US GPM, an operating capacity above 1,270 kg and a shatterproof cab. Read the skid steer sizing guide and confirm your exact machine with DTE.

Should I choose a smooth drum or a bite control drum?

A smooth drum costs less and is more productive with smaller material, but is more likely to jam on woody material. A bite control drum uses AR450 rings to limit bite depth, suits consistent heavy wood processing and needs less loader power.

Which teeth suit my ground conditions?

Steel planer teeth give the finest mulch but need regular sharpening and should be kept out of sand. Carbide planer teeth need no sharpening and produce slightly larger chips. Carbide hammer teeth suit rocky or sandy ground. Read DTE’s mulcher teeth guide.
