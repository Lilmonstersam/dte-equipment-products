DTE supplies two Virnig V70 forestry mulcher attachments for high-flow skid steers. The drum mulcher produces a finer, more contained mulch and grinds stumps below grade. The disc mulcher clears faster on open rural sites.

Choose by site and finish first, then match the attachment to your loader. Both mulchers have nearly identical hydraulic and machine-weight requirements, so the same skid steer specification applies to either. Ask DTE to confirm the fit for your machine.

Frequently asked questions

What is a forestry mulcher attachment?

A forestry mulcher is a hydraulic attachment that cuts standing trees, regrowth and scrub and mulches the material on site. The Virnig V70 drum and disc mulchers mount on high-flow skid steers and need a shatterproof cab on the loader.

What size skid steer do I need to run a mulcher attachment?

Virnig’s minimum is a rated operating capacity of at least 1,270 kg with forestry cab protection. DTE recommends a rated operating capacity of 1,350 kg or more, an operating weight of 4,500 kg or more and 115–190 L/min of high-flow hydraulics. Both V70 mulchers require a high-flow loader. Read the skid steer sizing guide and confirm your machine with DTE.

Which mulcher should I choose for stump grinding?

Choose the V70 Drum Mulcher. It can mulch about 2 inches below grade when rolled forward on its skid shoes. The disc mulcher cannot work below grade.
